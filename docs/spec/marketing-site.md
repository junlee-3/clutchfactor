# Marketing site — spec (2026-08-29)

> The public page for ClutchFactor v1.0.0. One page, one job: a CS2 player
> who reviews demos lands here, understands within seconds that this is a
> coach that shows its evidence — not a stats tracker — and downloads it.
> Direction chosen by the owner from three mocks (2026-08-29): **A "The
> Tape"** (the app's own navy studio extended to the web, the owner's
> gameplay clips as the hero) **with C's play ledger as the first section
> below the fold.** Hosting: `site/` in this repo, deployed to Vercel
> (ADR-0012).

## 1. Goal, audience, constraints

- **Audience:** CS2 players who want to improve and distrust "AI coach"
  claims. They know the vocabulary (trade, isolated, dying for free,
  util, entry) — the copy uses it without explaining it.
- **Job:** download v1.0.0. Secondary: visit the GitHub repo.
- **Voice:** the app's. Sentences, not slogans; numbers in mono; every claim
  on the page is something the app actually does today (README, v1.0.0).
  No fake match data — every insight, ledger row and stat on the page is
  copied from a real report/replay screenshot in `docs/screenshots/` or
  `docs/design/walkthrough-v1.3|v1.4/`.
- **Single dark theme,** like the app. No light mode.
- **Footage is the owner's.** The site never ships third-party gameplay.
  Until clips exist, the hero shows a poster; until a poster exists, a
  dimmed real radar (`de_inferno`). The page never looks broken.
- **URL:** `clutchfactor.vercel.app` until a domain is bought. The mocks'
  `clutchfactor.gg` was a placeholder, not a decision.

## 2. Where it lives, stack

```
site/
  index.html                 the page (semantic sections, real copy)
  src/main.ts                boot: hero clips, ledger, download CTA, reveals
  src/hero.ts                clip rotation + fallbacks (pure schedule fn + DOM glue)
  src/ledger.ts              ledgerSchedule(rows) → delays; DOM sequencing
  src/platform.ts            detectPlatform(ua, platform) → 'mac' | 'windows' | 'other'
  src/release.ts             version, asset file names, sizes, URLs — ONE place
  src/styles/tokens.css      copy of ../../src/styles/tokens.css color/space tokens
  src/styles/site.css        page styles (type scale, components, sections)
  public/clips/README.md     how to make clips (ffmpeg one-liner), naming
  public/clips/clips.json    manifest: [{ "file": "clip-01.mp4" }, …]
  public/clips/poster.jpg    (owner-supplied; fallback radar if absent)
  public/shots/*.webp        generated from docs/screenshots by `pnpm shots`
  public/og.jpg              1200×630, generated by `pnpm shots`
  public/favicon.svg         app-icon.svg
  scripts/shots.mjs          sharp: png → webp @1440 + @960, og.jpg
  test/*.test.ts             vitest: platform, ledger schedule, manifest, tokens drift
  package.json / pnpm-lock.yaml / tsconfig.json / vite.config.ts / eslint.config.js / vercel.json
```

- **Vanilla TypeScript + plain CSS, Vite.** No framework: the page's
  behaviour is clip rotation, a timed ledger, OS detection and scroll
  reveals — a few hundred lines. No hydration, no runtime dependency.
- **Standalone package** with its own lockfile; no pnpm workspace, so the
  Tauri app's install/build and its CI jobs are untouched. Root
  `eslint.config.js` adds `site/` to `ignores`; `site/` has its own config.
- **Tokens are copied, not imported** (Vercel builds from `site/` as root).
  `test/tokens.test.ts` parses both `tokens.css` files and asserts every
  color and spacing token matches — drift fails CI.

### 3.3 How it works — `#ledger` (the C section)

Split 42 / 58 on ≥ 1024 px, stacked below.

- Eyebrow **How it works** · `h2` **Every round, narrated.**
- Body: *Import a .dem. The engine rebuilds every round as a play ledger —
  setup, utility, trades, deaths, rotations, outcome — and gives each round
  a verdict. When one play decided it, the report names that play.*
- Ledger (left), header `ROUND 2 · INFERNO · CT` / `LOST · YOU 2–1 · 3v0`,
  rows copied from `docs/screenshots/replay.png`:

  | t | title | detail | edge |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:05 | Setup at CT spawn | Nearest teammate Roland Pryzbylewski, 432 u · 3 of 4 teammates within 900 u | — |
  | 0:31 | Rotated to the plant in 11 s | 2,320 u from the plant when it went down | — |
  | 0:55 | Killed Konky | 1,436 u · MP9 · +2% win probability · 3v5 before | win |
  | 1:01 | Killed MyUnit | 775 u · MP9 · +35% win probability · 3v2 before | win |
  | 1:12 | Round lost — bomb exploded | 1v0 at the end · 2 kills, 121 damage | — |
  | 1:12 | Death | Nearest: Roland Pryzbylewski · At Apartments · Not traded — after the round was decided | loss |

  Rows appear in sequence when the section enters the viewport (once):
  `ledgerSchedule(rows)` maps each row's timestamp to a delay — first row at
  400 ms, last at 3,600 ms, linear in the timestamp between; equal
  timestamps stagger by +250 ms. Under reduced motion all rows are visible
  immediately.
- Right: `shots/replay.webp` in a Frame; caption (mono, dim): *The 2D
  replay — positions, health, weapons, kill feed, callouts on the map. 60 fps,
  deep-linked from every claim.*

### 3.4 What it catches — `#catches`

- Eyebrow **What it catches** · `h2` **Stop dying for free.**
- Body: *Deaths are sorted into 15 classes by a rule engine over parsed
  events — positions, trades, utility, timing. Every rule carries a
  confidence, and rules bias toward silence: a missed detection is fine, a
  wrong accusation is not.*
- Left card **How you died** (from `report.png`, rendered as HTML bars, mono
  labels, bar = `--ink-dim`, the fair-duel bar = `--win`):
  Outaimed in a fair duel 7 · No-engagement death 5 · Self / world / teammate 2
  · Isolated & untradeable 1. Caption: *46.7% were fair duels you lost on
  mechanics — good news: the rest had a fixable cause.*
- Right: three insight cards (HTML, `--loss` 2 px left edge, confidence chip
  top-right, dashed evidence chips linking to `#download` with
  `title="Open the tape — in the app"`), verbatim from `report.png`:
  1. **Missed 4 trades in range** · 70% — *You were close enough to trade 4
     teammate deaths and stayed on your angle — rounds 3, 12, 13 and 16.
     Keep your crosshair where he is fighting so the trade is one step, not a
     repositioning job.* Chips R3 · 0:16, R12 · 0:28, R13 · 0:14, R16 · 0:16.
  2. **Died isolated twice** · 75% — *You died isolated twice with no
     teammate close enough to punish the kill — rounds 6 and 9. Take those
     duels one angle closer to a teammate: arrive together, or hold until
     someone can trade you.* Chips R6 · 0:23, R9 · 0:11.
  3. **You traded in, nobody followed** · 70% — *You committed to the trade
     and the follow-up never came — twice, rounds 6 and 9. You were the only
     one who re-peeked — Mashed Potato and Crunchy Potato were nearest and
     stayed put; that is a team spacing problem, not a reason to stop
     trading.* Chips R6 · 0:23, R9 · 0:11.
- Footnote under the cards (mono, dim): *Dashed means you can click through
  to the tape — in the app.*

### 3.5 The coach — `#coach`

- Eyebrow **The AI coach · optional** · `h2` **A coach that can't make up
  numbers.**
- Body: *With a Gemini key set, the coach reads the measured facts for a
  round and writes its own read — a judgment call, not a template fill-in.
  Every number, name and callout it cites is checked against those facts
  before you see it; anything that doesn't check out is rejected in favor of
  the template. No key means no network call and no coach — just the
  plain-language templates.*
- `shots/coach.webp` in a Frame (full width).
- Three facts (hairline-separated row, `h3` + one line):
  **Checked, not trusted** — every figure the coach cites is verified against
  the parsed demo. · **Local by default** — the only network call this app
  ever makes is to Google's Gemini API, and only with a key you set. ·
  **Bring your own key** — Settings → Coach; stored in the app's local
  database.

### 3.6 Honest limits — `#limits`

- Eyebrow **What your coach watches** · `h2` **What it can't see, it says
  so.**
- Body: *A dedicated screen lists every detection rule in plain language
  with its live thresholds, which of the 15 death classes aren't built yet
  and why, and what the engine flatly cannot see. Deaths it can't attribute
  stay "Unclassified", and the report says so.*
- Two lists side by side: **Not built (and why)** — Over-peeks (8) and wide
  peeks (10) need peek geometry the parser doesn't provide; per-death
  hotspot classification (12) needs a "standard angle" model — hotspots are
  tracked across matches instead. **Cannot see** — economy · utility lineups
  · comms · aim mechanics · line of sight.
- `shots/watches.webp` in a Frame.

### 3.7 Across your matches — `#habits`

- Eyebrow **Across your matches** · `h2` **Habits, not hot takes.**
- Body: *Patterns are promoted only when they repeat — "Left trades on the
  table in 5 of your last 10 matches" — including repeat death hotspots per
  map, with evidence into each contributing demo. Trends chart every habit
  across your imports and call out streaks, good and bad.*
- Two Frames: `shots/trends.webp` — *Trends: per-habit sparklines, the share
  of deaths that were pure aim duels, streak callouts.* ·
  `shots/corpus.webp` — *Reference corpus: drop pro demos from HLTV; with
  8+ on a map it flags spots you hold that pros rarely do — "unusual, not
  wrong".*

## 4. Visual system (web)

Tokens: identical names and values to `src/styles/tokens.css` §Neutrals,
§Ink, §Accent, §Game hues, §Space, §Radius, §Elevation, §Motion. Web-only
additions live in `site.css`, never in `tokens.css`:

```css
--font-sans: "Inter", -apple-system, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace;
--t-hero:  clamp(40px, 6vw, 68px) / 1.02;  /* 600, -0.03em */
--t-h2:    clamp(30px, 3.6vw, 44px) / 1.08; /* 600, -0.025em */
--t-h3:    20px / 1.3;                      /* 600 */
--t-lede:  19px / 1.5;
--t-body:  17px / 1.6;                      /* max 68ch */
--t-ui:    14px / 1.4;
--t-data:  13px / 1.4 mono;                 /* tabular-nums */
--t-micro: 11px / 1.3 mono;                 /* uppercase, .16em, --ink-dim */
--gutter:  clamp(20px, 4vw, 40px);  --container: 1200px;
--section: clamp(64px, 9vw, 112px);        /* vertical rhythm between sections */
```

- Fonts: Inter 400/500/600, JetBrains Mono 400/500 from Google Fonts —
  the app uses system stacks (ADR-0009); the web page loads the named faces
  so it looks the same on Windows. Fallback stacks declared.
- `--ink-faint` (#5c6672 on #0e1116 ≈ 3:1) is never used for text the
  reader needs; ledger timestamps and captions use `--ink-dim` (≈ 5.6:1).
- Components (one implementation each, in `site.css`): **Button** primary
  (accent fill, `#0b1118` ink) / secondary (`bg1` at 70%, `line-strong`
  border); **Chip** evidence (mono, dashed `--ink-dim` underline, hover
  `--ink-bright`) / confidence (mono, `bg2`, `r-sm`); **Card** (`bg1`,
  `line`, `r-md`, optional 2 px tone edge); **Frame** (screenshot window:
  `bg-tape`, `line-strong` border, `r-lg`, `0 24px 60px rgb(0 0 0 / .55)`);
  **Eyebrow**; **Ledger row** (grid `44px 1fr`, hairline top, 2 px tone
  edge for win/loss). Accent appears once per screen: the primary button.
- Focus: the app's one treatment — 2 px `--accent-bright` ring, 2 px offset,
  on every interactive element.
- Motion budget (everything else is static): clip crossfade 1,000 ms
  opacity; scroll reveals opacity + 8 px translateY over 200 ms `--ease`,
  once, via IntersectionObserver; the ledger sequence (§3.3); nav ground
  fade 200 ms; the `.is-target` ring on an evidence chip's card fading over
  1.2 s (§3.2). `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables all five and shows
  the poster instead of video.
- Breakpoints 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440. Below 720 px: poster only, hero
  stacks, buttons full-width, ledger above its screenshot. No horizontal
  scroll at any width; Frames are `max-width: 100%`.

## 5. Hero clips

- Owner records 3–5 clips of their own gameplay. Spec (in
  `public/clips/README.md`): H.264 MP4, 1280×720 or 1920×1080, 24–30 fps,
  8–15 s, no audio track, ≤ 5 MB each; ffmpeg one-liner provided
  (`-an -vf scale=1280:-2 -c:v libx264 -crf 26 -preset slow -movflags +faststart`).
  Plus `poster.jpg` (1920×1080, ≤ 300 KB): the first frame of clip-01.
- `clips.json` lists files in play order. Two `<video>` elements: the
  current one plays to `ended` (no `loop`), the next is preloaded
  (`preload="auto"` only for the next), then a 1 s crossfade; last → first.
  A single clip loops.
- Video is skipped (poster only) when any of: reduced motion,
  `navigator.connection.saveData`, viewport width < 720 px, manifest empty
  or fetch fails, or a clip errors (fall back to poster, log nothing).
- `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` on `/clips/*` and
  `/shots/*` (`vercel.json`); rename files to change them.

## 6. Download logic — `release.ts` + `platform.ts`

```ts
export const release = {
  version: "1.0.0",
  tag: "v1.0.0",
  mac:  { file: "ClutchFactor_1.0.0_aarch64.dmg",  bytes: 10549192, arch: "Apple silicon" },
  win:  { file: "ClutchFactor_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe", bytes: 7936273 },
  msi:  { file: "ClutchFactor_1.0.0_x64_en-US.msi", bytes: 10166272 },
};
export const assetUrl = (file: string) =>
  `https://github.com/junlee-3/clutchfactor/releases/download/${release.tag}/${file}`;
```

`detectPlatform(userAgent, maxTouchPoints = 0)`: `/Windows|Win64|Win32/` →
`windows`; `/Macintosh|Mac OS X/` without `/iPhone|iPad|iPod/` **and**
`maxTouchPoints ≤ 1` → `mac` (iPadOS in desktop mode reports `Macintosh`
but has touch points); everything else (iOS, Android, Linux) → `other`,
which falls back to Windows primary / macOS secondary (§3.2) so every
screen keeps one accent button. Pure, tested.
Sizes are rendered as whole MiB (`Math.round(bytes / 1048576)`) — the number Finder/Explorer show. Updating `release.ts` is a
line in CLAUDE.md's release checklist.

## 7. Deployment (Vercel) and CI

- Vercel project `clutchfactor`, Git-connected to `junlee-3/clutchfactor`,
  **Root Directory `site`**, framework Vite, install
  `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`, build `pnpm build`, output `dist`.
  Production branch `main`; every PR gets a preview URL. Ignored Build
  Step: `git diff HEAD^ HEAD --quiet -- .` (skips builds when `site/`
  didn't change). `vercel login` / `vercel link` are interactive — the
  owner runs them; the implementation plan stops there and says so.
- `site/vercel.json`: `cleanUrls: true`, the cache headers above, and
  security headers (`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`,
  `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`).
- CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`): new `site` job on `ubuntu-latest`,
  Node 22 + pnpm 10, `pnpm -C site install --frozen-lockfile`, then
  `typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build`. Not a required check until it has
  passed on main once (ruleset, ADR-0005).
- No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts. (Vercel Analytics is a
  one-line opt-in later if the owner wants counts.)

## 8. Testing and definition of done

TDD on the pure parts (vitest, `site/test/`):

- `platform.test.ts` — mac / windows / other from real UA strings,
  including iPadOS (reports `Macintosh`) → `other`.
- `ledger.test.ts` — `ledgerSchedule`: first 400 ms, last 3,600 ms, linear
  in between, +250 ms stagger for equal timestamps, single row → 400 ms.
- `clips.test.ts` — manifest validation: array of `{ file }` with `.mp4`
  names, empty allowed, anything else rejected (→ poster).
- `tokens.test.ts` — every token in `site/src/styles/tokens.css` except
  `--font-*` and `--text-*` (web type differs by design, §4) exists in the
  app's `src/styles/tokens.css` with the same value, and vice versa.

DoD, all evidenced in `docs/design/walkthrough-site/` (README + PNGs, per
the repo's sign-off habit):

1. `pnpm -C site typecheck && lint && test:run && build` green; CI `site`
   job green on the PR.
2. Headless renders at 375×812, 768×1024, 1440×900: no horizontal scroll;
   hero `h1` ≥ 40 px and the primary button above the fold at every size;
   nav readable over the poster.
3. Reduced-motion render shows the poster and every ledger row at once.
4. Lighthouse (on the Vercel preview, or against `pnpm -C site preview`
   if the project isn't linked yet): performance ≥ 95 without clips
   (≥ 90 with), accessibility ≥ 95, best practices ≥ 95, SEO ≥ 95.
5. Every download URL in `release.ts` answers `200` (`curl -sI`).
6. Screenshot alt text describes what is on screen (not "screenshot").
7. CLAUDE.md gains the `site/` map line, `pnpm -C site dev`, and the
   `release.ts` step in the release checklist; stays ≤ 120 lines.
